Lattice privacy policy

Last updated 19 October 2026.

Lattice keeps your data on your iPhone. It has no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no ads, and it does not sell or share your data.

What Lattice stores

Your tasks, projects, habits, journal entries and voice recordings are saved in a database on your iPhone, inside the app. Lattice also keeps a local log of which features are used (for example, "a task was moved"), which never contains your text and never leaves the device. Deleting the app deletes all of this.

Apple Calendar

If you allow calendar access, Lattice reads and writes events through iOS. Your events stay in Apple Calendar; Lattice shows them but does not copy them into its own database.

Microphone

If you record a voice clip in the journal, Lattice uses the microphone only while you record. Recordings are saved on your iPhone.

Google Calendar

Google Calendar is off until you turn it on in Settings and sign in with Google. Lattice then asks for:

  • Your Google account's email address and basic profile, to show which account is connected.
  • Access to your Google Calendar (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar), to list your calendars, show their events on Lattice's timeline, and create, edit and delete events when you ask it to.

How this data is handled:

  • Requests go directly from your iPhone to Google. Lattice has no server, so your Google data is never sent to the developer or anyone else.
  • Events are held in the app's memory while you view them. They are not saved in Lattice's database, and they are not used for anything except showing and editing your calendar in the app.
  • Your Google sign-in is kept in the iOS Keychain by Google's sign-in library.
  • Lattice does not use Google data for advertising, does not sell it, does not transfer it to anyone, and does not use it to train AI or machine-learning models.
  • You can disconnect at any time: Settings > Google Calendar > Sign out. You can also remove Lattice's access from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Lattice's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

App updates

Lattice checks for app updates from Expo's update service, which receives standard technical information (such as the app version and platform) to deliver them. It receives none of your content.

Changes

If Lattice ever adds a feature that sends your data off your iPhone, such as syncing between devices, this policy will be updated before that feature ships, and the feature will say what it sends.
