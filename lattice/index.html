Lattice

A calendar, task list, habit tracker and journal for iPhone, in one place.

What it does

  • Plan your day on a timeline, with your tasks, habits and calendar events side by side.
  • Keep projects and tasks, and drag them onto the day.
  • Track habits, and write or record a journal entry for each day.
  • Show and edit events from Apple Calendar and, if you choose, Google Calendar.

Google Calendar

Lattice can connect to your Google Calendar so your events appear on its timeline and you can create, change and delete them from the app. You turn this on in Settings and can sign out at any time. Lattice reads and writes events directly between your iPhone and Google; it has no servers of its own. See the privacy policy for exactly what is accessed and why.

Privacy

Everything you put into Lattice stays on your iPhone. Read the privacy policy.
